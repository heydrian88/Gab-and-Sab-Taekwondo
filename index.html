Gabriel "Gab"

Philippine Taekwondo Association (PTA) Registered

Known for his crisp form execution, balance and high speed roundhouse. A gold medalist in WATA Open International Taekwondo Championship which was held in Japan. Gab is currently qualified to participate in the Batang Pinoy National Finals 2026.

Weight Division: Welterweight (-48kg)
Favorite Kick: Roundhouse / Dollyeo
